GrainLab

Food Science Compiler for Home Bakers

🔄 Workflow Overview

1. Manage Inventory

Start by adding your fresh-milled grains and kitchen equipment to your Inventory. GrainLab uses AI to analyze your wheat berries and suggest optimal hydration, gluten potential, and culinary uses. It also logs your equipment so the recipe engine knows what tools you have available.

2. Compile Recipe

Head to the Compiler to build your recipe. Initialize the category you want to make in phase 1, then choose an archetype to have the AI generate recipe ideas for you in phase 2. Once you select a recipe, the AI will then recommend the types of grains you have that fit that recipe.

3. AI Processing & Baking

Phase 3 will have the AI determine the rest of the ingredients required to make the recipe. You'll be able to look at any substitutions if necessary. In Phase 4, the AI evaluates your specific grain mix and your available equipment to generate a tailored step-by-step process. It will tell you exactly when to use your stand mixer, how to shape your dough, and what sensory cues to look out for.

🧠 Recommended AI Settings

GrainLab is designed to run locally using the Ollama API, keeping your data private and avoiding API costs.

  • Recommended Model: Gemma4:E4B - All of the development was done for Gemma4:E4B, and the prompts were tailored to allow small models to reliably generate quality responses.
  • Additional LLM Recommendations: It's recommended to have a smaller context window , 16K, and have multiple slots available. The prompts are designed to be specific and fire simultaneously to prioritize quality over speed. Having only one slot in your configuration will cause the prompts to be evaluated in order, which will take more time.
  • Hardware Requirements: Requirements vary based on the type of model you choose to run. A dedicated GPU with 12GB of VRAM is recommended. Testing was done using an RTX 3080Ti with 12GB of VRAM.
  • Configuration: This app was designed for local LLMs using Ollama/llama.cpp, and is intended to be used without an API key. Theoretically, any endpoint with an OpenAPI standard API should work, but cloud models have not been tested. Your mileage may vary. You can verify your connection and select your model in the Settings page.
